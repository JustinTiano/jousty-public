# Jousty ⚔️

**The Discord bot for [The Joust](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4744160/The_Joust/).**
Bring The Joust's lobbies and leaderboards into your server.

## [➕ Add Jousty to your server](INVITE_LINK)

## What it does

- **Looking for group:** when a player shares a public lobby from The Joust, Jousty posts it in
  your LFG channel with a **Join game** button and pings the members who opted in. The post
  disappears when the invite expires.
- **Live leaderboards:** one scoreboard of every leaderboard's top 5 for today, updated live,
  with an alert when someone takes #1. At 8 PM the day's board becomes the final and a fresh
  one starts.
- **Opt-in pings:** a 🔔 button lets members choose whether they get pinged. Nobody is pinged
  unless they opted in.

## Setting it up

1. Add Jousty with the link above.
2. Run `/lfg-setup` and pick the channel for lobbies (Jousty creates the ping role and the 🔔
   panel).
3. Run `/leaderboard-setup` and pick the channel for the scoreboard, and optionally a separate
   channel for #1 alerts.

Both commands are for people with **Manage Server**. Anyone can use `/leaderboard` to see a
board's top 10, privately.
