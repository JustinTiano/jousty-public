# Jousty Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 7, 2026*

Jousty keeps as little as it can, only what it needs to post in your server, and never sells
or shares it.

## What Jousty stores

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your server's ID, and the IDs of the channels, roles and Jousty's own messages chosen or posted during setup | so it knows where to post, which role to ping, and which of its own messages to update or delete |
| Lobbies shared from The Joust (host name, player count, game type, join link) | to post them; kept for 7 days |
| Public leaderboard scores from The Joust's leaderboard service (player names, Steam IDs, scores) | to draw the scoreboard and alerts; kept for 40 days |
| Technical logs (server IDs, errors) | to keep the bot running; old logs are overwritten automatically |

In The Joust's own community server only, Jousty also runs bug reports and suggestions, which
keep the reporter's Discord user ID with each report. No other server has those features.

## What Jousty does not do

- It does **not** read your messages. It doesn't have the Message Content or Server Members
  permissions (privileged intents).
- It does **not** store members' names, profiles or who clicked its 🔔 button. That button just
  adds or removes a role, and Discord keeps track of roles.
- It does **not** send DMs, sell data, or share it with anyone.

## Deleting data

When Jousty is removed from a server, everything it kept for that server (its settings and the
records of its posts there) is deleted automatically. Shared game data (lobbies and scores) isn't
tied to any server and is deleted on the schedule above.

To ask a question about your data, or to have something removed sooner, ask in the
[Jousty support server](https://discord.gg/d4Bsa4Qkb).

## Where it's stored

On a private server in the United States, reachable only by its operator.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes.
